# Excel-Sales-and-Finance-Analytics
This Excel-based project simulates a real-world business intelligence report for AtliQ Hardware, a company operating in the computers and accessories domain. The objective was to extract meaningful insights from raw sales and finance data to support strategic business decisions.

**Business Context:**

AtliQ distributes products through multiple channels such as Croma, Flipkart, Amazon, Best Buy, and more. To optimize performance and growth, they needed in-depth visibility into their sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and financial health across global markets.

**🚀 Project Objectives & Outcomes:**
- Built dynamic customer performance and market comparison reports to highlight high-performing regions and customers.

- Identified top 5 countries based on net sales and discovered new product launches by fiscal year.

- Created Profit & Loss dashboards segmented by fiscal year, month, market, and product.

- Evaluated Gross Margin %, COGS, and Profitability across multiple business dimensions (quarters, regions, and categories).

- Implemented a custom fiscal calendar (Sep–Aug) for proper year segmentation.

**🧠 Key Learnings & Value to Business:**

- Helped identify underperforming markets and potential expansion opportunities.

- Improved understanding of how discounts and sales trends impact revenue and profitability.

- Enabled better strategic planning and forecasting by revealing key financial KPIs.

- Made it easier for stakeholders to track performance metrics through visually intuitive pivot dashboards.

**🔧 Technical Highlights:**
✅ ETL Process using Power Query: Cleaned and transformed raw data from multiple tables (e.g., dim_product, dim_market, fact_sales_monthly).

✅ Data Modeling: Created star schema by building one-to-many relationships between dimension and fact tables in Power Pivot.

✅ Fiscal Date Logic: Generated a date table with calculated fiscal months, quarters, and years using custom DAX.

✅ Calculated Measures: Used Excel formulas and DAX to compute net sales, gross margin, profit/loss, and year-wise trends.

✅ Interactive Reports: Created Pivot Tables and Charts for real-time slicing and dicing of data.

**💡 Skills Demonstrated:**

- Power Query Editor & Data Cleaning

- Pivot Table/Chart Visualization

- Advanced Excel Functions: XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, SUMIFS, AVERAGE, MEDIAN, MODE

- DAX for calculated columns & fiscal logic

- Power Pivot Data Model Design

- Business Analysis & Data Interpretation
